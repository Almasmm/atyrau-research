
Case Study Task 1
Assessment and Analysis of Air Pollution Levels in Major Cities of Kazakhstan

1. Background
Air pollution is one of the most pressing environmental and public health challenges facing Kazakhstan. Rapid urbanization, industrial expansion, coal-based energy production, transportation emissions, and heating systems significantly contribute to deteriorating air quality. Cities such as Almaty, Astana, Temirtau, Karaganda, and Ust-Kamenogorsk frequently record high levels of particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10), nitrogen dioxide (NO₂), sulfur dioxide (SO₂), and other pollutants.
2. Objective of the Case Study
Your task is to investigate the state of air pollution in selected Kazakhstan’s cities, identify major contributing factors, and propose data-driven recommendations to mitigate pollution.
Key objectives:
Analyze real or publicly available air quality datasets from Kazakhstan.
Compare pollution levels across different cities.
Identify primary pollution sources (industry, energy, transport, climate factors).
Evaluate seasonal patterns (winter peaks, heating season).
Assess health and environmental risks.
Propose evidence-based solutions.
3. Research Questions
Which Kazakhstani cities experience the highest levels of air pollution?
What pollutants (PM2.5, PM10, SO₂, NO₂, CO, O₃) are most problematic?
What are the main sources of air contamination in each city?
How do seasonal changes affect pollution levels?
What measures can realistically reduce pollution in the next 5–10 years?
4. Data Requirements
Use one or more of the following sources:
Open datasets:
AirKaz.org – real-time air quality data
Kazhydromet – official environmental monitoring reports
IQAir / World Air Quality Index
Local government reports or academic publications
Data to collect:
Pollution metrics: PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃
Temperature, wind, humidity (for seasonal analysis)
Industrial output, transport density (optional)
5. Methodology
You may use the following steps:
5.1 Data Collection
Upload or scrape available air-quality datasets.
Clean missing or inconsistent readings.
5.2 Data Analysis
Perform:
Time-series analysis
Seasonal decomposition
Correlation with weather or industrial activity
5.3 Visualization
Create:
Line charts of pollutants over months
Heatmaps comparing cities
Bar charts ranking pollution sources
AQI maps (optional)
5.4 Evaluation
Compare results to World Health Organization (WHO) air quality standards.
Identify high-risk periods (e.g., winter inversion in Almaty).
Quantify exceedance days.
5.5 Recommendations
Provide:
Short-term actions (transport policy, heating regulations)
Long-term strategies (green energy transition, urban planning)
